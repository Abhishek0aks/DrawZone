# User Guide

**Goal:** earn the most points by guessing words quickly and by drawing so others can guess.

1. **Start.** Open the game. Wait until the top right says *Connected*.
2. **Create a room.** Type a nickname (2 to 20 characters) and press *Create room*. You become the host.
3. **Invite friends.** Tell them the 5-character room code (press *Copy room code*). They type a nickname and the code, then press *Join room*.
4. **Start the game.** The host presses *Start game* when at least 2 players are in the room.
5. **If you are the drawer.** Choose one of three words within 15 seconds. Draw it with the mouse or your finger. Use the colors, brush size, eraser and *Clear canvas*. Do not write the word.
6. **If you are a guesser.** Watch the canvas. The row of underscores shows how many letters the word has. Type your guess in the box and press *Guess*. Faster correct guesses give more points (up to 100).
7. **After each turn** you see the word, who guessed it and the scores. The next player draws.
8. **At the end** the final ranking appears. Ties share the same rank. The host can press *Play again*; anyone can press *Return to home*.

Tips: the drawer earns 20 points for every player who guesses correctly. You cannot join a game that has already started. If you lose your connection you leave the room and must join again.
